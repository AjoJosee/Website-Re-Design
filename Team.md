## About Your Redesign

### What did you change?

- **Neo-Brutalist Craft Paper Visual Language**: Replaced generic corporate UI with a warm parchment and craft-paper aesthetic inspired by `nothingonpurpose.com` and the official *TinkerHub CET Designers' Handbook*.
- **Official Brand Compliance**: Implemented the exact neutral, primary, secondary, and accent color scheme from the handbook (`#1A1F20`, `#FFFFFF`, `#205B67`, `#FFCD10`, `#95BF15`, `#05BFCE`, `#0060FF`, `#EE1700`) with required 10% logo padding.
- **Interactive Certificate of Certified Tinkerer Generator**: An interactive tool allowing students to personalize, stamp (with real rubber stamp physics and audio), and print their own official Tinkerer Pass.
- **Handbook-Compliant Status & Poster Lab**: A live interactive creator enforcing the handbook's 720×1280 Instagram/WhatsApp status specs, drop shadows, and alternating Light/Dark checkerboard rules.
- **Community & Culture Spotlight**: Added an archival scrapbook honoring previous Execom members (Sudev Suresh, Nanda Kishor, Nandana Sreeraj, Vishnu Prakash) and real hackathon projects (BitFlip winners Webster-14, Decentralwood, ALS Assistive device).
- **Tactile Micro-interactions & Playful Easter Eggs**: Draggable floating stickers with pointer physics, custom Web Audio API sound effects, particle confetti bursts, and cheeky Rickrolls that give the site a youthful, fun personality.

### Why did you make these changes?

The original website was standard and static, failing to convey the electric energy, maker spirit, and peer-to-peer warmth that defines TinkerHub at CET. The new redesign focuses on **fun over sterile efficiency**, bringing students into an interactive playground where design rules from the 2021 handbook come alive through touch, sound, color, and laughter.

### Key Features

* **Interactive Certificate of Certified Tinkerer**: Instant live personalization, branch selection, rubber-stamp sound & visual slam, plus printable pass.
* **Handbook Poster & Status Lab**: Interactive 720×1280 status preview with exact Photoshop drop shadow specs and checkerboard theme alternation.
* **Draggable Sticker Playground**: Touch- and pointer-enabled stickers with bounce, snap, and sound effects.
* **Zero-Dependency Web Audio Synthesizer**: Generates mechanical clicks, pops, stamp thuds, fanfare, and chiptune tunes directly in-browser.
* **Maker Kootam Project Showcase**: Real community projects with persisted live upvote counters and confetti celebrations.
* **Light / Dark Theme Switcher**: Toggleable theme strictly respecting the handbook's `#1A1F20` / `#FFFFFF` checkerboard rule.
* **Cheeky Student Easter Eggs & Rickrolls**: Humorous traps ("Free Canteen Samosa Pass", "KTU S-Grade Script", retro TV player) celebrating student hacker culture.

### Technology Used

* HTML5 (Semantic & Accessible)
* CSS3 (Custom properties, neo-brutalism, washi tape, rubber stamps, responsive grid/flexbox)
* Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+, Web Audio API, Canvas Confetti, Pointer Events, LocalStorage)
* Google Fonts (Patrick Hand, Caveat, Special Elite, Montserrat, Space Grotesk)
