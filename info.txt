Website Re-Design Challenge
Drishti 2026 x Tinkerhub CET


The Challenge

Redesign the TinkerHub CET website and reimagine how the community could be represented on the web.

The goal is to create a website that feels fun, creative, youthful and experimental, while still being professional and easy to navigate.

Reference Websites

Existing TinkerHub CET website:
https://tinkerhubcet.github.io/

TinkerHub:
https://tinkerhub.org/

Use these as references for understanding the existing identity and community. You are encouraged to take the visual language in a new direction rather than simply recreating the current website.


Design Direction

The website should:

- Retain a visual connection to the existing TinkerHub CET colour theme.
- Feel fun and experimental, while maintaining a professional finish.
- Have a strong and creative landing page.
- Use interesting interactions, animations or visual elements where appropriate.
- Represent the community and its people, not just its activities.
- Have a clear and intuitive user experience.

Suggested Sections


 Landing Page: Create an engaging and memorable first impression of TinkerHub CET.

About: Introduce TinkerHub CET, what it stands for and what the community does.

Events / Activities: Showcase the events, workshops, hackathons, sessions and other activities conducted by the community.

Maker Kootam/Projects: Highlight projects and things built by the community.

Into the History: Create a section dedicated to the history of TinkerHub CET, including previous Execom/Core teams and the people who have contributed to the community over the years.

Current Team: Introduce the current Execom/Core.

Contact: Provide ways for visitors to get in touch and find TinkerHub CET online.

Get Creative

You are encouraged to add your own sections and ideas.

Some possibilities:

- Interactive community timeline
- TinkerHub archive
- Member stories
- Fun interactive elements
- Easter eggs
- Mini-games
- Experimental navigation
- Creative project showcase
- "TinkerHub culture" section

There is no fixed structure beyond the suggested sections.

General Guidelines

- Prioritize originality and creativity.
- Keep the website responsive across desktop and mobile.
- Maintain good readability and usability.
- Don't simply copy the existing TinkerHub CET website or other references.
- Use animations  and interactions purposefully.
- Keep the design consistent with the TinkerHub CET identity.
- The final website should feel like a real, polished website, not just a design mockup.
